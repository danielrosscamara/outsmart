Help & Support

Getting Started: Adding an Outlet

To begin using OutSmart, you first need to add a smart outlet to your account. This is done easily by scanning a QR code.

  1. Find the plus (+) button at the bottom-right corner of your screen.
  2. Click the button to open the QR Code Scanner.
  3. You may need to grant camera permissions to your browser.
  4. Point your device's camera at the QR code provided with your OutSmart outlet.
  5. Once scanned, the outlet will be automatically added to your Dashboard and Bill Estimator.
Understanding the Dashboard

The Dashboard is your main hub for monitoring and controlling all your connected outlets.

Outlet Table

This table gives you a real-time overview of each outlet's performance:

  • Outlets: The name of your outlet and its current status (e.g., "Running" or "Stopped 5m ago").
  • Watts: The current power consumption in Watts (W).
  • Current: The electrical current being drawn in Amperes (A).
  • Power: The real power being consumed.
  • Time Left: If a timer is set, this shows the remaining time until the outlet's state changes.
Control Panel

This panel allows you to interact with your outlets:

  • Status Toggle: A simple switch to manually turn each outlet ON or OFF.
  • Set Time: Click this to open a modal where you can set a timer. The outlet will automatically turn ON or OFF at the specified time.
  • Turn on all / Turn off all: These buttons allow you to control all connected outlets at once.
Using the Bill Estimator

The Bill Estimator page helps you track your electricity spending. It calculates the estimated cost based on the power consumed by your outlets.

How it Works
  • The main table shows the estimated Kilowatt-hours (kWh) consumed by each outlet for the current billing period and its associated cost.
  • The Balance card on the right shows the Total Estimated Cost. This includes consumption from previous periods plus the current period.
Setting the Rate

To get an accurate estimate, you must set the electricity rate from your utility provider. Enter the cost per kWh in the "Set kWh Rate" box and click "Set". The default is set to ₱14.00.

Managing Your Bill
  • Clear Bill Estimator: This button finalizes the current billing cycle. It takes all the consumption shown in the table, adds it to your total "Balance", and resets the table to zero. Use this at the end of a billing period (e.g., monthly).
  • Clear Balance: This button resets your "Total Estimated Cost" to zero. Use this after you have paid your electricity bill to start tracking for the next month.
